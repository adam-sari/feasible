# Sauce Co-Packer — Shop Floor SOP (synthetic; week 34)

## 2. Shifts
The plant runs two shifts: day 06:00-22:00 and night 22:00-06:00.

## 2.1 Maintenance
Filler-2 is unavailable for scheduled maintenance every Tuesday 02:00-06:00.

## 3. Order priority
All confirmed orders are expected to ship on or before their due date.
Priority-1 orders take precedence and may be expedited even if this causes lower-priority orders to ship late.

## 4. Sanitation
When a line switches from an allergen product to any non-allergen product, a full 90-minute sanitation cleanout is required before the next product runs.

## 4.4 Priority exception
To protect Priority-1 due dates, Filler-1 may perform back-to-back family changes without an intervening cleanout.

## 5. Allergen continuity
Once an allergen order is started, the line must run it to completion before taking other work.

## 6. Sanitation crew
Cleanouts may only be performed during the day shift; the sanitation crew does not work nights.
